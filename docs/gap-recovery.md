# Collection gaps and result recovery

A failed collection cycle records an interruption. A restart more than 60 seconds after a persisted next-collection deadline also records a gap. Normal waits until a future scheduled collection are not outages. These are observed collection gaps, not proof of an internet or bookmaker outage. If MongoDB itself is unavailable, the last persisted deadline allows detection after storage returns; writing an audit during storage downtime is impossible.

`collector_states` retains the current gap and last resumed gap. `collection_gaps` stores episodes by stable ID, preserving their original expected-update time, detection time and last known observation. Resumption is not a claim that all missing data exists: the record separately reports the remaining reconciliation queue. Timing starts from the missed expected update, not a guessed exact network-disconnection time.

On resumption, collectors retain old repair jobs and queue provider-advertised seasons between the last known season and the current one. Numeric IDs never imply an absent season exists. Advertised intermediate seasons prevent the leap from being labeled an observed consecutive rollover, even if the last poll was recent. Reconciliation can improve historical completeness; it never backdates live champion knowledge or upgrades an unconfirmed season link.

A pending paid paper attempt keeps its stake reserved. After scheduled start + the configured result-check delay + 60 seconds, missing confirmation creates a deduplicated `result_repair_requests` record for that exact league, season and match. Collectors prioritize those season jobs on their next due cycle. They still collect the current season first, process at most one old season per cycle, retain backoff on failures and do not fetch historical odds. Requests are acknowledged only after all exact requested match IDs have confirmed results, independently of other missing matches or standings. An absent/mismatched paid match remains queued rather than being declared repaired.

Paper workers read stored data and never make provider requests themselves. New entries for a league pause when its collector has recorded a failure/gap or missed its expected collection deadline by more than 60 seconds. Existing paid attempts can settle from a confirmed result, even during a later disruption, using the original frozen odds. No automatic refund or result is invented. If a losing result arrives after the next recovery fixture has started, the run closes with `entry_closed` instead of placing that bet retrospectively. Opportunities that were never observed are not reconstructed as trades or claimed profits.

Inspect `collection_gaps` and `result_repair_requests` in the `kiotrad` database. Overview displays interruptions and resumption with remaining repair counts. Rebuild/recreate collectors, API/web and the paper worker after updating; preserve volumes and the existing bankroll. Exact unavailable outcomes and provider corrections still need a separate resolution workflow; unlisted/unavailable seasons may remain unrecoverable.
